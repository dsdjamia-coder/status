Our Objective & Purpose

campaign.dsd provides decentralized, high-fidelity marketing tools for modern community and institutional events. We believe direct student, alumni, or patron participation is the most powerful organic visual reach. We aim to secure, simplify, and scale the status generation process.

Privacy Policy & Secure Data Handling

We hold our users' data privacy in the absolute highest regard. The photos and metadata uploaded into campaign.dsd during the poster generation flow are processed completely in the local browser canvas memory. We do not persist or store the participant photos or names onto any central server. All administrative operations are fully secured using high-grade Firebase authentication.

Terms of Use
